# Priority inbox mockup

Open [index.html](index.html) in your browser. The file includes its styles, JavaScript, and
icons, so it works offline without a build or server.

Click **All** to cycle through **Unread**, **Read**, and back to **All**. Try it with the project
picker and the **Starred** filter. Click **Priority** or **Everything else** to collapse or
expand that section. Hover over a row to reveal its star at the far left and Snooze on the right.
Colored stars stay visible when you move away. Right-click a row to mark it unread or settle it.

The mockup uses sample threads. Reload the page to reset them.
